2 Information Collection & Local Storage

No Account or Personal Registration Required: You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, photos, or contacts to play BRAINFLIP: Memory Match. We do not operate external backend servers that store your personal identity.

Locally Stored Gameplay Data

To provide a seamless gaming experience across Single Player (1,000 levels) and 2-Player Versus modes, the app stores game progress locally on your device using Android's standard SharedPreferences storage:

  • Level Progress & Stars: Your current level, highest unlocked level, and 1-to-3 star ratings earned on completed levels.
  • Unlocked Card Collection & Hints: Cards unlocked during gameplay and your available hint balance.
  • App Preferences: Sound effects mute/unmute toggle and game configuration settings.

This gameplay data remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted to the Developer.

3 Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

BRAINFLIP: Memory Match uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements inside the app, specifically:

  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen ads displayed periodically between completed levels (after every 2 completed levels).
  • Rewarded Ads: Optional video ads that you can choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards (such as recharging +1 Hint).

Data Collected by Google AdMob

To serve relevant ads, limit repetitive ads (frequency capping), detect fraud, and measure ad performance, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain information from your device, including:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID / AdID) and app set ID.
  • IP Address & Coarse Location: Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general/approximate geographical location (city/country level).
  • Device & Diagnostic Information: Device make and model, operating system version, screen size, language setting, mobile network carrier, app version, and crash/performance diagnostics.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Information about advertisements shown in the app, including ad impressions, clicks, and rewarded video completions.

Managing or Opting Out of Personalized Ads

You can reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time on your Android device by navigating to: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and selecting "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

4 Device Permissions Used

BRAINFLIP: Memory Match requests only the minimum install-time Android permissions necessary for core gameplay and ad delivery. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos/media, or precise GPS location.

Permission Purpose in App
android.permission.INTERNET Required to request and load Interstitial and Rewarded ads from Google AdMob.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used to check whether an active internet connection is available before loading ads.
android.permission.VIBRATE Used to provide subtle tactile feedback during card flips and gameplay interactions.

5 Data Retention & Deletion

Because the Developer does not collect or store any personal user accounts on external servers, all of your game progress (levels, stars, hints, and settings) is stored locally on your device.

  • Clear Local Data Anytime: You can permanently erase all saved game data at any time by opening your device's Settings → Apps → BRAINFLIP: Memory Match → Storage and tapping Clear Data / Clear Storage.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application from your device removes all locally stored progress and preferences.
  • Reset Advertising ID: You can delete or reset your Google Advertising ID at any time via your Android device's Google Settings.
